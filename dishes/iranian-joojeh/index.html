Iranian Joojeh Kabab

Next stop: Omani Shuwa · continuing the voyage

Pronounced joo-JEH kah-BAHB

Saffron chicken with lemon and onion, charred at the edges.

30 min prep · 15 min cook · Serves 4 · 4 skewers

History

Joojeh kabab is saffron chicken on skewers, a standard Iranian grill beside the minced lamb. Saffron is the point, bloomed and rubbed into the meat, not a thread scattered on the plate after cooking. Lemon and onion are the usual partners. The dish is poultry and is cooked through. This version stays dairy-free. A dairy marinade would be a different recipe.

Cultural background

Iranian kebab cooks grill joojeh for people who want chicken rather than lamb. Without saffron it is just grilled chicken, so the spice is not optional on this page.

Why it tastes this way

Saffron's floral, slightly bitter note clings to chicken fat and smells stronger once the surface browns. Lemon keeps it sharp. Onion sweetens the marinade. Charcoal adds smoke that a pan cannot. The color is a side effect of the flavor, not the reason to buy the spice.

How it is made

Marinate chicken in bloomed saffron, lemon, and onion, then grill on skewers until the poultry is cooked.

Authentic touch

Grind the saffron with a pinch of salt and bloom it in warm lemon juice before it touches the chicken. Do not toast it until it tastes like hay.

How it is used

With rice and grilled tomato.

Field notes

  • Joojeh kabab is Iranian saffron chicken on skewers.
  • Saffron is the point of the marinade, not a last-minute garnish.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless chicken thigh, cut in cubes
  • 1/2 teaspoon saffron threads
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 small onion, grated
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 metal skewers

Steps

  1. Grind the saffron with a pinch of the salt and stir it into the lemon juice. Mix with onion, the rest of the salt, and oil. Add the chicken and refrigerate 30 minutes.
  2. Thread onto skewers. Heat the grill to medium-hot.
  3. Grill, turning, until the thickest cube reaches 165°F (74°C).
  4. Rest a few minutes and serve with rice. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.

Cook poultry to 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part, not touching bone.

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Thighs instead of breast, which stay moister after they reach temperature.
  • A dairy marinade, which would be a different recipe from this dairy-free one.

Pairings

  • Rice
  • Grilled tomato
  • Lemon
