Maine Grilled Lobster

Next stop: Washington Half-Smoke · continuing the voyage

Sweet lobster meat with a charred shell and melted butter.

15 min prep · 12 min cook · Serves 2 · about 2 servings

History

Maine's lobster ports, Rockland among them, boil lobster as a matter of course, and they also split it and set it on a grill. Grilling is one coastal way to cook it, not a replacement for the boiled lobster. The meat is done when it is opaque.

Cultural background

Split lobster over a hot grill. It is a short cook of a live lobster you kill and split just before the fire, or that a fishmonger splits for you the same day.

Why it tastes this way

The shell chars and the meat steams inside it, so you get a little smoke without drying the tail if you pull it off in time. Butter is the coastal table habit. Cold North Atlantic lobster is the ingredient; the grill is only the last step.

How it is made

Grill split lobster shell-side down, then flesh-side, until the meat is opaque.

Authentic touch

Have the lobster split the day you cook it. Do not grill a lobster that sat split and warm.

How it is used

In the shell, with butter and lemon. Picks and a cracker belong on the table.

Field notes

  • Grilling a split lobster is a Maine coast method alongside boiling.
  • The meat is done when it turns opaque and pearly, not when the shell merely smells smoky.

Ingredients

  • 2 live lobsters, about 1 1/4 pounds each, split lengthwise the day you cook them
  • 2 tablespoons butter, melted
  • 1 lemon, halved
  • A hot grill

Steps

  1. Ask the fishmonger to split the live lobsters the day of cooking, or split them immediately before the grill if you are equipped to do that humanely and safely. Remove the stomach sac and the intestinal vein.
  2. Heat the grill hot. Lay the halves shell side down for a few minutes.
  3. Turn and brush the meat with butter. Cook until the flesh is opaque and the thickest meat reaches 145°F (63°C).
  4. Serve with lemon. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.

Cook shrimp, lobster, and crab until the flesh is opaque and pearly. An internal temperature of 145°F (63°C) is the target for lobster and shrimp.

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Tails alone grill faster than a whole split lobster. Still check the temperature.
