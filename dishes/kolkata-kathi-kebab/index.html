Kolkata Kathi Kebab

Next stop: Malai Chicken Tikka · continuing the voyage

Pronounced KAH-tee keh-BAB

Skewered lamb with yogurt, ginger, and chili, browned over a hot fire.

30 min prep · 18 min cook · Serves 4 · about 4 servings

History

Kathi kebab is the skewered meat of a Kolkata street plate. In the city it is often rolled into a paratha and eaten as a kathi roll. This recipe cooks only the meat. The paratha stays a pairing, not an ingredient, so the kebab has no wheat if the marinade has none; yogurt, ginger, and chili are the marinade; the lamb is whole muscle on a skewer, from an Indian kitchen in Kolkata.

Cultural background

Kolkata street cooks grill this meat and, when you ask, wrap it. The written dish is the skewer. Yogurt means dairy. There is no flour in the bowl, which is deliberate so the meat can be gluten-free.

Why it tastes this way

A hot skewer browns the yogurt marinade on small pieces of lamb, and ginger and chili stay bright. The Kolkata roll adds a toasted paratha around that meat. Leaving the paratha on the side lets you taste the kebab itself, which is the grilled part of the street plate.

How it is made

Marinate lamb in yogurt and spice with no wheat, grill it on skewers, and rest it. Offer a paratha beside it if you want the roll.

Authentic touch

Cut the lamb small enough to cook through on a street-style skewer. Huge chunks are a different roast.

How it is used

Eat the meat with onion and lemon. A paratha can sit on the side as a pairing and is not part of the marinade.

Field notes

  • In Kolkata the kebab is often eaten in a paratha roll. The bread is a pairing on this page.
  • The marinade has no wheat, so the meat itself can be gluten-free.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 pounds boneless lamb, cut in 1-inch chunks
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon grated ginger
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon Kashmiri chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon neutral oil

Steps

  1. Mix the yogurt, ginger, garlic, chili, cumin, salt, lemon, and oil. Do not add wheat flour. Coat the lamb and refrigerate it. Do not leave garlic in oil at room temperature. The lamb is still raw. Cook whole cuts of beef, pork, lamb, or goat to at least 145°F (63°C) and rest 3 minutes.
  2. Thread the lamb on skewers. Heat a grill until it is hot. Cook whole cuts of beef, pork, lamb, or goat to at least 145°F (63°C) and rest 3 minutes.
  3. Grill, turning, until the center of the thickest chunk reaches at least 145°F (63°C). Cook whole cuts of beef, pork, lamb, or goat to at least 145°F (63°C) and rest 3 minutes.
  4. Rest the meat 3 minutes. Serve the kebab with onion and lemon. If you add a paratha, keep it as a side. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours. Cook whole cuts of beef, pork, lamb, or goat to at least 145°F (63°C) and rest 3 minutes.

Cook whole cuts of beef, pork, lamb, or goat to at least 145°F (63°C) and rest 3 minutes. A shoulder you want to pull can continue toward 195°F (90°C).

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Chicken pieces are also sold on Kolkata skewers and would need a poultry temperature. This is lamb.
  • Wrapping the meat in a paratha makes the street roll and adds wheat.
