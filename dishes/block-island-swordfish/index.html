Block Island Grilled Swordfish

Next stop: Vermont Maple-Cider Chicken · continuing the voyage

Meaty swordfish with salt, oil, and a clean grill mark.

10 min prep · 10 min cook · Serves 4 · about 4 servings

History

Block Island, off Rhode Island, has long been a port for offshore swordfish boats. Grilled steaks are a summer plate when the fish is landed. The cook is short. Swordfish dries out if it is left on the grate past the point where it turns opaque.

Cultural background

Grilled swordfish steaks. A New England port fish, cooked simply. It is not a smoked fish and not a chowder.

Why it tastes this way

Swordfish is dense and mild, so salt, oil, and a hot grate show the meat instead of a sauce. A short cook keeps the center moist. The island's place in the fishery is why the plate is tied to this harbor rather than to a generic grill.

How it is made

Oil and salt swordfish steaks and grill them briefly on both sides.

Authentic touch

Use steaks about an inch thick. Thin pieces overcook between the time you turn them and the time you plate them.

How it is used

Whole steaks with lemon. A green salad or corn fits beside them.

Field notes

  • Block Island is a Rhode Island port long tied to offshore swordfish landings.
  • Swordfish steaks turn dry if they cook far past the point where the center is opaque.

Ingredients

  • 4 swordfish steaks, about 1 inch thick
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 lemon

Steps

  1. Oil, salt, and pepper the steaks. Heat the grill hot and oil the grate.
  2. Grill about 4 minutes on the first side, then turn.
  3. Cook until the center reaches 145°F (63°C) and the flesh is opaque.
  4. Serve with lemon. Do not hold the steaks over the fire once they flake.

Cook fish to 145°F (63°C), until it is opaque and flakes.

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A spoon of capers and lemon is a later sauce. This recipe stops at oil, salt, and lemon.
