Argentine Chimichurri

History

Chimichurri is the green sauce of the Argentine asado: parsley, oregano, garlic, vinegar, oil, and chile. Stories about the name are disputed, so this page does not pick one. It is a table sauce, stirred and rested, not a cooked glaze. It belongs beside grilled beef, and it is not the Dominican wasakaka or the Nicaraguan garlic spoon.

Cultural background

There is no meat, egg, dairy, or wheat in the bowl. It is vegetarian and vegan. Garlic in oil should be refrigerated and used within a few days. Do not leave it at room temperature.

Why it tastes this way

Vinegar and raw garlic cut the fat of grilled beef, and parsley and oregano keep the sauce green and herbal. Dried chile adds a small heat without making a pepper paste. On a Río de la Plata table the sauce is the contrast to salted meat and wood coals.

How it is made

Chop parsley, oregano, garlic, and chile, then stir them with vinegar, oil, and salt.

Authentic touch

Chop the parsley by hand if you can. A blender makes a bitter, uniform puree that is a different sauce.

How it is used

Spoon it over sliced grilled beef, or serve it in a bowl so people take what they want.

Field notes

  • The sauce is parsley, oregano, garlic, vinegar, oil, and chile.
  • Stories about the name are disputed, so this page does not choose one.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup finely chopped fresh parsley
  • 1 tablespoon dried oregano
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/4 cup red wine vinegar
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon dried chile flakes
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Chop the parsley and mince the garlic. There is no meat in this sauce.
  2. Stir in oregano, vinegar, oil, chile flakes, and salt.
  3. Taste for salt. Let it sit 15 minutes in the refrigerator so the garlic softens into the vinegar.
  4. Serve cold or cool. Refrigerate leftovers and use them within a few days. Do not leave garlic in oil at room temperature. This is not a canning recipe.

These ingredients are vegetables, bread, or a sauce. Keep them clean, and refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours. This is not a canning recipe.

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A red chimichurri adds tomato and more chile. This one stays green.
  • Red wine vinegar and white vinegar are both used. This recipe uses red wine vinegar.

Pairings

  • grilled beef
  • grilled sausage
  • bread
