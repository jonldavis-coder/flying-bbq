Minnesota Grilled Walleye

Next stop: Michigan Smoked Whitefish · continuing the voyage

Mild, sweet walleye with butter, salt, and a light char.

10 min prep · 10 min cook · Serves 4 · about 4 servings

History

Walleye is the state fish of Minnesota, and grilled fillets are a standard lake-country plate in summer. The fish is lean and mild, so the cook is short and the seasoning is light. This is a fillet on a grate, not a Door County boil and not a smoked whitefish.

Cultural background

Grilled walleye fillets with butter. A Minnesota lake plate. Keep the fish cold until it goes on the fire.

Why it tastes this way

Walleye is lean, so butter and a hot grate add the richness the fillet does not have on its own. Overcooking makes it cottony, which is why the plate is a quick grill rather than a smoke. Northern lakes are the reason the fish is on the table at all.

How it is made

Grill walleye fillets over a hot fire until they flake.

Authentic touch

Oil the grate and turn the fillet only once. Walleye sticks and then breaks if you fuss with it.

How it is used

Whole fillets with lemon. Potatoes or a green salad sit beside them. Butter is in the recipe.

Field notes

  • Walleye is the state fish of Minnesota.
  • The fillet is lean, so a short grill and a little butter keep it from drying.

Ingredients

  • 4 walleye fillets, about 6 ounces each
  • 1 tablespoon butter, melted
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 lemon

Steps

  1. Pat the fillets dry. Brush with butter and season with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat a grill hot and oil the grate.
  3. Grill, turning once, until the thickest part reaches 145°F (63°C) and the fish is opaque and flakes.
  4. Serve with lemon. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.

Cook fish to 145°F (63°C), until it is opaque and flakes.

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A broiler can finish the same fillets if a grill is impossible. Use the same temperature.

Pairings

  • lemon

  • green salad
