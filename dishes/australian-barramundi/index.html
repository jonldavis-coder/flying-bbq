Grilled Australian Barramundi

Next stop: Australian Damper · continuing the voyage

Pronounced ba-ruh-MUN-dee

Barramundi fillets with salt, lemon, and a little oil, grilled until they flake.

15 min prep · 12 min cook · Serves 4 · about 4 servings

History

Barramundi is a well-known Australian fish, Lates calcarifer, caught and farmed in the north, including waters around Darwin. Fillets are grilled with salt and lemon and served the day they are cooked. The flesh is mild and is ready when it reaches 145°F (63°C) and flakes. This is a northern Australian fish grill, not a chili curry and not a smoked whole fish.

Cultural background

Cooks in Darwin and other northern towns grill barramundi at home and in small restaurants. There is fish, salt, and lemon. There is no wheat, dairy, or peanut. The skin can stay on if you like it crisp.

Why it tastes this way

Barramundi is mild and a little sweet, so salt, lemon, and a hot grate are enough. The skin, if you leave it on, crisps and protects the flesh. Overcooking dries it, which is why the temperature matters more than a heavy sauce. The northern coast and the rivers are where the fish lives. The plate is that fish and the fire.

How it is made

Salt barramundi fillets and grill them until the center is opaque and flakes.

Authentic touch

Oil the grate and start skin-side down if the skin is on. Turning too soon tears the fillet.

How it is used

Eat the fish with lemon, a salad, and potatoes or rice.

Field notes

  • Barramundi is an Australian coastal and river fish, often grilled as a fillet.
  • The flesh is cooked to 145°F (63°C), until it flakes.

Ingredients

  • 4 barramundi fillets, about 6 ounces each
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon neutral oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 lemon, cut in wedges
  • 2 tablespoons chopped parsley

Steps

  1. Pat the fillets dry and rub them with salt, pepper, and oil. The fish is still raw. Cook fish to 145°F (63°C) until it is opaque and flakes.
  2. Heat a grill and oil the grate. Cook fish to 145°F (63°C) until it is opaque and flakes.
  3. Grill, turning once, until the center reaches 145°F (63°C) and the flesh is opaque and flakes. Cook fish to 145°F (63°C) until it is opaque and flakes.
  4. Serve with lemon and parsley. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours. Cook fish to 145°F (63°C) until it is opaque and flakes.

Cook fish to 145°F (63°C), until it is opaque and flakes.

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A whole small fish can be grilled if it fits the grate. Check the temperature at the thickest point.
  • Dill or parsley on top is a fresh finish and does not change the cook.

Pairings
