Croatian Raznjici

Next stop: Bosnian Cevapi · continuing the voyage

Pronounced RAZH-nyee-tsee

Pork cubes with paprika, garlic, and a light grill char.

20 min prep · 15 min cook · Serves 4 · 4 skewers

History

Ražnjići are pork or veal skewers grilled across Croatia and the neighboring Balkans. They are everyday fire food rather than a single-town secret. This recipe uses pork shoulder cubes with paprika and garlic, which is a common home mix. Veal is the other usual meat and can replace the pork if you keep the same temperature for a whole-muscle cut. They are not minced ćevapi.

Cultural background

Croatian households cook these on a backyard grill. The page is a skewer of whole muscle. It is not a sausage and not a long roast.

Why it tastes this way

Pork shoulder cubes brown at the edges and stay moist if the pieces are even. Sweet paprika colors the surface without making the skewer a chile dish. Garlic and a hot grate are enough. The same shape of skewer shows up across the region because it is a practical way to cook small pieces over coals.

How it is made

Season pork cubes, thread them, and grill over a hot fire until the center is safe, then rest.

Authentic touch

Cut the cubes the same size so they finish together. Oil them so the paprika does not scorch into bitterness.

How it is used

With chopped onion, ajvar if you have it, and a green salad. Ajvar is a pepper relish kept on the side.

Field notes

  • Ražnjići are pork or veal skewers grilled as everyday food in Croatia.
  • They are whole pieces of meat, not the minced sausages called ćevapi.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb pork shoulder, cut in 1 inch cubes
  • 1 tablespoon sweet paprika
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 4 metal skewers

Steps

  1. Toss the pork with paprika, garlic, oil, and salt. Thread onto skewers.
  2. Heat the grill to a hot coal bed.
  3. Grill, turning, until the thickest cube reaches at least 145°F (63°C). Give the meat a 3-minute rest.
  4. Serve with onion. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.

Cook whole cuts of beef, pork, lamb, or goat to at least 145°F (63°C) and rest 3 minutes. A shoulder you want to pull can continue toward 195°F (90°C).

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Veal cubes in the same marinade, still a whole-muscle cut.
  • A milder mix with only salt, pepper, and oil.

Pairings

  • Chopped onion
  • Ajvar
  • Green salad
