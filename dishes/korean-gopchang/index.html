Korean Gopchang

Next stop: Korean Moksal · continuing the voyage

Pronounced GOP-chahng

Grilled beef intestine with salt, garlic, and sesame oil, chewy and rich.

25 min prep · 20 min cook · Serves 4 · about 4 servings

History

Gopchang is beef small intestine, grilled at Korean barbecue restaurants that specialize in offal. The pieces are cleaned, often briefly boiled, and then grilled with salt, garlic, and sesame oil. It is a regular part of Korean meat grilling, cooked for the texture and the fat, not as a stunt. Beef offal is cooked and rested; sesame oil means sesame is in the dish.

Cultural background

Specialist restaurants and some home cooks grill gopchang on a hot plate. Buy it fresh and cleaned from a butcher you trust. There is beef and sesame. There is no wheat and no dairy in this seasoning. Cook it the day you buy it.

Why it tastes this way

Intestine is mostly texture and fat. A hot grill browns the fat, salt and garlic season it, and sesame oil adds a toasted smell. A short boil before the grill renders some of the richness and makes the pieces easier to cook evenly. Dipping sauces at a Korean table then cut that fat with salt and chili.

How it is made

Rinse cleaned beef intestine, simmer it until it is safe, then grill it with garlic and sesame oil and rest it.

Authentic touch

Trim and rinse carefully, and skip any piece that smells sour. Strong spice will not fix a bad piece of offal.

How it is used

Eat it hot with a salt-and-pepper dip, lettuce, and rice.

Field notes

  • Gopchang is beef small intestine grilled at Korean offal barbecue restaurants.
  • Beef offal is cooked to at least 145°F (63°C) and rested.

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds cleaned beef small intestine
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • water to cover, for the simmer

Steps

  1. Rinse the intestine in cold water and trim anything discolored. If it smells spoiled, discard it. The offal is still raw. Cook beef offal to at least 145°F (63°C) and rest 3 minutes.
  2. Simmer it in water until the thickest part reaches at least 145°F (63°C). Drain and cool it enough to handle. Cook beef offal to at least 145°F (63°C) and rest 3 minutes.
  3. Toss it with sesame oil, garlic, salt, and pepper. Grill over high heat until browned, and confirm the center is still at least 145°F (63°C). Cook beef offal to at least 145°F (63°C) and rest 3 minutes.
  4. Rest it 3 minutes. Serve hot and refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours. Cook beef offal to at least 145°F (63°C) and rest 3 minutes.

Cook beef offal to at least 145°F (63°C) with a rest. Cook poultry offal, including chicken hearts and livers, to 165°F (74°C).

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Pork intestine is a different dish. This page is beef.
  • A chili paste on the side raises the heat. The grill seasoning here is mild.
