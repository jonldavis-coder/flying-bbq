Hungarian Fatanyeros

Next stop: Bulgarian Meshana Skara · continuing the voyage

Pronounced FAW-tahn-yeh-rohsh

A platter of grilled pork chops and garlicky sausage with paprika and browned fat.

25 min prep · 25 min cook · Serves 4 · 4 servings

History

Fatányéros is a Hungarian mixed grill served on a wooden platter. The name refers to a wooden board. Restaurants and home cooks pile several meats on it, often pork and sausage, with salad beside. This recipe uses pork chops and a fresh pork sausage so each can be cooked to its own temperature. It is a platter, not one cut with a single story of origin.

Cultural background

Hungarian hosts put this out when several people are eating from one board. It is not a goulash and not a single sausage. Pork and sausage are both on the fire, and they do not share one doneness.

Why it tastes this way

The platter works because the meats taste different: a chop has a seared fat edge, and a sausage has garlic all the way through. Paprika, used as a Hungarian table spice rather than a huge chile load, colors the pork. Wood or charcoal smoke ties the board together. Cooking each piece to its own temperature is what keeps the chop juicy and the sausage safe.

How it is made

Grill pork chops and fresh sausages over the same fire, taking temperatures separately, and serve them together on a board.

Authentic touch

Start the sausage earlier than the chops. Pull each item when it is ready instead of waiting for the thickest thing on the grill.

How it is used

On a wooden board with salad, pickles, and potatoes. The board is the old serving habit.

Field notes

  • Fatányéros is a Hungarian mixed grill, and the name refers to a wooden platter.
  • Pork chops and sausages on the same board need different finished temperatures.

Ingredients

  • 4 pork chops, about 1 inch thick
  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tablespoon sweet paprika
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
  • 1 tablespoon oil

Steps

  1. Mix the ground pork with half the garlic, half the paprika, and 1/2 teaspoon salt. Form four sausages. Rub the chops with oil, the remaining garlic, paprika, and salt.
  2. Heat the grill. Start the sausages over medium heat.
  3. Cook the sausages, which are ground meat, to 160°F (71°C). Cook the pork chops, a whole cut, to at least 145°F (63°C) and give them a 3-minute rest.
  4. Set both on a board. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.

Cook whole cuts of beef, pork, lamb, or goat to at least 145°F (63°C) and rest 3 minutes. A shoulder you want to pull can continue toward 195°F (90°C).

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A slice of beef steak added for a larger board, cooked as a whole muscle.
  • Shop-made fresh sausage if you do not grind your own, still checked with a thermometer.

Pairings

  • Potatoes
  • Pickles
  • Green salad
