Pit-Side Baked Macaroni and Cheese

Next stop: Brunswick Stew · continuing the voyage

Baked macaroni in a cheese custard, browned on top.

25 min prep · 35 min cook · Serves 8 · about 8 servings

History

Baked macaroni and cheese is a standard side at Southern barbecue pits, scooped next to pork or brisket. This version is baked in an oven, not smoked, and it contains no bacon, so it is vegetarian. The pasta is wheat, the sauce is milk and cheese, and an egg sets the custard.

Cultural background

An oven-baked pit side. Vegetarian because there is no bacon. Not vegan, not gluten-free, and not dairy-free. It is not a smoker recipe.

Why it tastes this way

Cheese and milk make a rich custard that bakes onto the pasta, and a hot oven browns the top so the dish has a crust. Beside salty smoked meat, that richness is the contrast pits are after. It earned a place on the tray as a Southern supper dish long before it was a restaurant extra.

How it is made

Boil macaroni, fold it into a cheese and egg custard, and bake it.

Authentic touch

Bake it until the center is hot and the egg is set. A pale, loose pan is not finished, and a dry one has gone too long.

How it is used

Warm, in squares or scoops, beside barbecue. No bacon is added.

Field notes

  • This macaroni and cheese is baked in an oven. It is not put in the smoker.
  • There is no bacon in the dish, so the written recipe is vegetarian.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound elbow macaroni
  • 2 cups milk
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F (177°C). Boil the macaroni in salted water until just tender, then drain it.
  2. Heat milk and butter until the butter melts. Take the pan off the heat. Beat the eggs and stir in a little hot milk to warm them, then stir the eggs back into the milk with the cheese, salt, and pepper.
  3. Fold in the macaroni, pour it into a buttered baking dish, and bake until the center reaches 165°F (74°C) and the top is browned. That temperature sets the egg.
  4. Rest 10 minutes before serving. There is no bacon. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.

These ingredients are vegetables, bread, or a sauce. Keep them clean, and refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours. This is not a canning recipe.

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A different sharp cheese changes the flavor. Keep the milk, butter, and egg if you want this custard.

Pairings

  • chopped pork
  • brisket
  • greens
