Lebanese Shish Taouk

Next stop: Lebanese Kafta · continuing the voyage

Pronounced sheesh TA-ook

Chicken with yogurt, garlic, lemon, and a light char.

30 min prep · 15 min cook · Serves 4 · 4 skewers

History

Shish taouk is chicken on skewers, a standard of Lebanese grills. A yogurt marinade is common, which means the dish contains dairy and is not vegan. Garlic and lemon sharpen it. The pieces are small so they cook quickly over charcoal. It is poultry, and it has to be cooked through. This is not lamb kafta and not a garlic sauce by itself.

Cultural background

Lebanese restaurants and home cooks grill taouk for a weeknight or a spread. The yogurt makes it dairy. Toum, the garlic sauce, is a separate emulsion and is not in this recipe.

Why it tastes this way

Yogurt clings to chicken and browns in spots, while lemon and garlic stay bright if you do not burn them. Charcoal adds smoke the oven misses. The dairy is part of the flavor, not a garnish, which is why a dairy-free claim would be false for this version. Small cubes cook evenly, which suits breast or thigh if the temperature is checked.

How it is made

Marinate chicken in yogurt, garlic, and lemon, then grill on skewers until the poultry is cooked through.

Authentic touch

Use thigh meat if you can, and wipe off thick yogurt before the grill so it does not carbonize. Check the center of a cube, not the skewer.

How it is used

With salad, pickles, and rice. A garlic sauce on the side is optional and, if it contains egg, is a separate allergen.

Field notes

  • Lebanese shish taouk is chicken on skewers, often in a yogurt marinade.
  • Yogurt means the dish is dairy and not vegan.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb boneless chicken thigh, cut in cubes
  • 1/2 cup plain yogurt
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 lemon, juiced
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 4 metal skewers

Steps

  1. Mix the yogurt, garlic, lemon juice, salt, and oil. Add the chicken and refrigerate 30 minutes.
  2. Thread onto skewers, letting extra yogurt drip off. Heat the grill to medium-hot.
  3. Grill, turning, until the thickest cube reaches 165°F (74°C), not touching bone if any bone remains.
  4. Rest a few minutes. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.

Cook poultry to 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part, not touching bone.

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Breast meat, watched closely so it does not dry after it reaches temperature.
  • A dairy-free oil marinade, which would be a different recipe and is not this one.

Pairings

  • Rice
  • Pickles
  • Tomato salad
