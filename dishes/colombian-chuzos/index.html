Colombian Chuzos

Next stop: Venezuelan Carne en Vara · continuing the voyage

Pronounced CHOO-sos

Pork skewers with cumin, garlic, and a little tomato.

25 min prep · 20 min cook · Serves 6 · about 6 servings

History

Chuzos are Colombian street skewers, usually pork or chicken, grilled and often served with potato and a sauce. They are not Peruvian anticuchos, which are beef heart, and they are not the large beef of the llanos. This recipe is pork, a whole muscle, cooked to 145°F (63°C). Chicken on the same stick would be a different temperature.

Cultural background

The marinade is garlic, cumin, oil, and a little tomato paste. It is a street seasoning, not a long cure. Arepa or potato can sit beside the skewer. Arepa is a pairing and, if made with wheat, would add gluten. Corn arepas do not. They are not in the ingredient list.

Why it tastes this way

Small cubes brown on every side, so the cumin and garlic crust is a large part of each bite. Charcoal is the street fire. The pork stays mild and a little sweet if you do not push the chile, which lets the potato beside it matter.

How it is made

Marinate pork cubes, skewer them, and grill them to a whole-pork temperature.

Authentic touch

Cut the pork evenly. A skewer with one thick cube and several thin ones will not finish at one temperature.

How it is used

Eat them with boiled potato and a simple aji or tomato sauce.

Field notes

  • Chuzos are Colombian street skewers, usually pork or chicken, not beef heart.
  • They are a different skewer from Peruvian anticuchos.

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds pork shoulder, in 1 1/2 inch cubes
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 1 tablespoon neutral oil
  • 1 tablespoon vinegar
  • Metal skewers

Steps

  1. Mix garlic, cumin, salt, tomato paste, oil, and vinegar. Coat the pork. It is raw and is not ready to eat. Thread it onto skewers. Whole cuts are not ready to eat until they reach at least 145°F (63°C) and rest 3 minutes.
  2. Heat a grill to medium-high. Whole cuts are not ready to eat until they reach at least 145°F (63°C) and rest 3 minutes.
  3. Grill, turning, until the center of the thickest cube reads 145°F (63°C). Rest the skewers 3 minutes.
  4. The pork should have reached 145°F (63°C) before the rest. Serve it hot. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.

Cook whole cuts of beef, pork, lamb, or goat to at least 145°F (63°C) and rest 3 minutes. A shoulder you want to pull can continue toward 195°F (90°C).

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Chicken chuzos use the same seasoning and must reach 165°F (74°C). This written recipe is pork.
  • A piece of sausage on the stick is ground meat and would need 160°F (71°C). It is not in this recipe.

Pairings

  • boiled potatoes
  • aji
  • arepa
