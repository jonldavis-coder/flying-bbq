Peshawar Chapli Kebab

Next stop: Nepali Sekuwa · continuing the voyage

Pronounced CHUP-lee keh-BAB

A wide, thin beef cake with tomato, cilantro, green chili, and cumin, fried on a griddle.

25 min prep · 20 min cook · Serves 4 · about 4 servings

History

Chapli kebab is a Peshawar dish of Pashtun cooks: minced beef, shaped wide and flat, and pan-fried on a griddle. Tomato, cilantro, and green chili are mixed into the meat. Cornmeal is the binder in this recipe so the cakes hold without wheat. It is ground beef and has to be cooked through; it is a Pakistani kebab from Peshawar, cooked on a plancha, not on a tandoor skewer.

Cultural background

Peshawar grill shops and home kitchens fry these cakes in shallow oil on a wide pan. There is no yogurt and no wheat in this written recipe. It is not a seekh and not a Balochistan sajji.

Why it tastes this way

A wide, thin cake gets crust on both sides, and the beef fat fries the edges. Tomato and cilantro stay bright inside. Green chili is the heat. That griddle fry is why chapli tastes different from a charcoal skewer of the same mince.

How it is made

Mix ground beef with tomato, chili, and cornmeal, shape thin cakes, and fry them on a griddle until the center is done.

Authentic touch

Shape them wide and thin. A thick cake browns outside and stays undercooked in the middle unless you cook it much longer.

How it is used

Eat them hot with onion, lemon, and a sliced tomato.

Field notes

  • Chapli kebab is associated with Peshawar and with Pashtun cooking there.
  • The cakes are wide and pan-fried, not molded onto a skewer.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 pounds ground beef
  • 1 tomato, seeded and finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup chopped cilantro
  • 2 green chilies, minced
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tablespoons fine cornmeal
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon ground coriander
  • 1.5 teaspoons salt
  • 3 tablespoons neutral oil, for the griddle

Steps

  1. Mix the beef, tomato, cilantro, chili, onion, cornmeal, cumin, coriander, and salt. Do not add wheat flour. The meat is still raw. Cook ground meat to 160°F (71°C).
  2. Shape eight wide, thin cakes. Heat a griddle with the oil over medium heat. Cook ground meat to 160°F (71°C).
  3. Fry until both sides are browned and the center of each cake reaches 160°F (71°C). Cook ground meat to 160°F (71°C).
  4. Rest them 3 minutes. Serve hot and refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours. Cook ground meat to 160°F (71°C).

Cook ground meat, including fresh sausages, to 160°F (71°C).

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Wheat flour is a common binder and would add gluten. This recipe uses cornmeal.
  • An egg is sometimes mixed in and would add egg. This batch has none.

Pairings

  • sliced onion
  • lemon
  • tomato
