Kelantan Ayam Percik

Next stop: Cebu Lechon · continuing the voyage

Pronounced AH-yum per-CHIK

Grilled chicken basted with a coconut sauce of lemongrass, chili, and turmeric.

30 min prep · 40 min cook · Serves 4 · about 4 servings

History

Ayam percik is a Kelantan grilled chicken, basted with a coconut-milk sauce while it cooks. Lemongrass, chili, garlic, and turmeric flavor that sauce. Kota Bharu and the villages around it are the place the dish is tied to. The chicken is grilled, not boiled in the sauce; coconut milk is not dairy, and this recipe leaves out shrimp paste and nuts.

Cultural background

Kelantanese cooks grill the chicken and brush the sauce on so it clings and browns. There is chicken in the dish, and no peanut, candlenut, dairy, or wheat in this written sauce. The heat is medium.

Why it tastes this way

Coconut milk, reduced on the skin, tastes toasted and slightly sweet, and lemongrass and turmeric keep it fragrant. Chili warms the baste. If the sauce only sits in a bowl, you miss the grilled crust that makes percik different from a coconut stew.

How it is made

Simmer a coconut, lemongrass, and chili baste, then grill chicken while brushing the sauce on, until the thigh is done.

Authentic touch

Baste in thin layers. A thick flood of sauce puts the fire out and peels off.

How it is used

Eat it with rice and a little of the remaining sauce.

Field notes

  • Ayam percik is Kelantan grilled chicken with a coconut baste.
  • The sauce is brushed on during grilling so it browns on the skin.

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken, about 3.5 pounds, cut into pieces
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 1 stalk lemongrass, smashed
  • 2 hot chilies, blended
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ground turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon sugar

Steps

  1. Simmer the coconut milk, lemongrass, chili, garlic, turmeric, salt, and sugar for 10 minutes, until slightly thick. Cool it. Do not leave the garlic sauce out for hours. Cook poultry to 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part, not touching bone.
  2. Coat the chicken with half the sauce and refrigerate it. The chicken is still raw. Cook poultry to 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part, not touching bone.
  3. Grill over medium heat, basting with the rest of the sauce, until the thickest piece reaches 165°F (74°C), away from the bone. Do not serve it rare. Cook poultry to 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part, not touching bone.
  4. Rest a few minutes and serve. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours. Cook poultry to 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part, not touching bone.

Cook poultry to 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part, not touching bone.

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Shrimp paste is used by some cooks and would add shellfish. It is not in this pot.
  • A hotter chili raises the baste. This one is medium.

Pairings

  • rice
  • cucumber
  • lime
