Grilled Oscypek

Next stop: Hungarian Fatanyeros · continuing the voyage

Pronounced oss-TIP-ek

Salty smoked sheep cheese with a browned face and a soft center.

5 min prep · 6 min cook · Serves 4 · 4 servings

History

Oscypek is a protected smoked sheep cheese from the Polish Tatra, made in the highland dairy tradition around Zakopane. Grilling thick slices is a common way to eat it at street grills in that region. The cheese is already salted and smoked before it meets the fire. It is a dairy food and not vegan. Cranberry sauce is sometimes served beside it and is not required for the grill itself.

Cultural background

Highland shepherds and the Zakopane grills that buy their cheese are the source. This page only grills the cheese. It does not teach you to make a protected cheese at home.

Why it tastes this way

Sheep milk is tangy, and the earlier smoke is already in the cheese. A short, hot grill softens the interior and browns the face without melting it into the coals if the slice is thick. The mountain dairy and the second kiss of heat are both in the bite. It is food from a herding landscape, not a garnish.

How it is made

Grill thick slices of oscypek over a hot fire until they brown and soften. Do not try to produce the protected cheese yourself.

Authentic touch

Cut the slices about half an inch thick and grill them dry or with a film of oil. Thin slices fall through the grate.

How it is used

Hot, as a side, with cranberry or alone. It is vegetarian and not vegan.

Field notes

  • Oscypek is a protected smoked sheep cheese from the Polish Tatra.
  • Grilling slices is a highland way of serving it, and the cheese is dairy, not a vegan food.

Ingredients

  • 12 oz oscypek or another smoked sheep cheese, in a thick piece
  • 1 teaspoon oil
  • Black pepper
  • A pinch of salt only if the cheese is mild

Steps

  1. Cut the cheese into slices about 1/2 inch thick. Oil them lightly.
  2. Heat a grill until the grate is hot.
  3. Grill until the faces brown and the center softens. There is no animal-protein thermometer target. Do not leave it so long that it collapses into the fire.
  4. Serve hot. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours. This is not a canning recipe.

Variations

  • A related smoked sheep cheese if real oscypek is not sold where you are, named as a stand-in, not as oscypek.
  • A minute under a broiler if you have no grill.

Pairings

  • Cranberry sauce
  • Boiled potatoes
  • Pickles
