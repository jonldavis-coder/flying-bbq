Japanese Sanma Shioyaki

Next stop: Hokkaido Jingisukan · continuing the voyage

Pronounced SAHN-mah shee-oh-YAH-kee

Salt-grilled Pacific saury, oily and direct, with grated daikon and lemon.

15 min prep · 12 min cook · Serves 4 · about 4 servings

History

Sanma no shioyaki is Pacific saury grilled with salt. In Japan it is an autumn fish, grilled at home and in small restaurants when the catch is in. Kesennuma and other ports land it. The method is salt and a hot grill, not a soy glaze; the flesh is cooked until it flakes; grated daikon and a citrus wedge are the usual plate, to cut the oil.

Cultural background

Home cooks salt the fish and grill it under a broiler or over a grate. There is fish and salt, and no wheat, dairy, or sesame in this recipe. It is a seasonal salt grill, not kabayaki.

Why it tastes this way

Saury is very oily, so salt and char are enough. The skin crisps and the oil bastes the flesh. Grated daikon is sharp and watery, which cuts that oil the way a heavy sauce would not. Out of season the fish is simply harder to find. The recipe does not change.

How it is made

Salt Pacific saury and grill it until the center is opaque, then serve it with grated daikon.

Authentic touch

Salt it shortly before grilling and dry the skin. A long salty soak makes a different, firmer fish.

How it is used

Eat it with rice, grated daikon, and a lemon or sudachi wedge.

Field notes

  • Sanma shioyaki is Pacific saury grilled with salt, an autumn fish in Japan.
  • Grated daikon is served beside it to cut the oil.

Ingredients

  • 4 whole Pacific saury, cleaned, or 4 saury fillets
  • 2 teaspoons salt
  • 1 cup grated daikon radish, for serving
  • 1 lemon, cut in wedges

Steps

  1. Pat the fish dry and salt both sides. Refrigerate it for 15 minutes. The fish is still raw. Cook fish to 145°F (63°C) until it is opaque and flakes.
  2. Heat a grill and oil the grate lightly. Cook fish to 145°F (63°C) until it is opaque and flakes.
  3. Grill, turning once, until the center reaches 145°F (63°C) and the flesh is opaque. Cook fish to 145°F (63°C) until it is opaque and flakes.
  4. Serve with grated daikon and lemon. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours. Cook fish to 145°F (63°C) until it is opaque and flakes.

Cook fish to 145°F (63°C), until it is opaque and flakes.

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A broiler can stand in for a grill if you watch the skin. Name it as a broiler.
  • Mackerel can be salted the same way when saury is not in the market. It is a different fish.

Pairings

  • rice
  • grated daikon
  • lemon
