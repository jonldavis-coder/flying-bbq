Japanese Tsukune

Next stop: Taiwanese Grilled Sausage · continuing the voyage

Pronounced tsoo-KOO-neh

Grilled chicken meatballs on skewers, seasoned with soy, ginger, and scallion.

25 min prep · 15 min cook · Serves 4 · about 4 servings

History

Tsukune is ground chicken shaped on a skewer and grilled at a yakitori shop. It sits beside the salted thigh skewers, and it is often finished with tare. Ground chicken has to be cooked through, to a higher temperature than a chop. This recipe puts a little soy sauce and egg in the mix, so it contains soy, wheat, and egg; it is a Tokyo yakitori-shop meatball, cooked the day it is mixed.

Cultural background

Yakitori cooks grill tsukune over the same charcoal as the other skewers. There is chicken, egg, soy, and wheat. There is no dairy. It is not a beef kebab and not a cured sausage.

Why it tastes this way

Grinding the chicken spreads soy, ginger, and scallion through the bite, and the grill browns the outside. Egg helps the mince hold. A tare, if you brush it on, adds sweetness at the end. The charcoal bitterness on the crust is the shop flavor, which a boiled meatball does not have.

How it is made

Mix ground chicken with egg, soy, and ginger, shape it on skewers, and grill it until the center reaches the temperature for ground poultry.

Authentic touch

Keep the ovals small and press out air pockets. A thick ball burns outside and stays under temperature inside.

How it is used

Eat the skewers hot, with rice. A tare from the separate recipe can be brushed on if you want it glossy.

Field notes

  • Tsukune is the ground-chicken skewer at a yakitori shop.
  • Ground poultry is cooked to 165°F (74°C).

Ingredients

  • 1.5 pounds ground chicken
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon grated ginger
  • 2 scallions, minced
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon neutral oil

Steps

  1. Mix the chicken, egg, soy sauce, ginger, scallions, salt, and oil. The chicken is still raw. Keep it cold. Cook ground poultry to 165°F (74°C).
  2. Shape small ovals on skewers. Heat a grill. Cook ground poultry to 165°F (74°C).
  3. Grill, turning, until the center of each oval reaches 165°F (74°C). Do not serve ground chicken rare. Cook ground poultry to 165°F (74°C).
  4. Rest a minute and serve. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours. Cook ground poultry to 165°F (74°C).

Cook ground poultry and sausages made from it to 165°F (74°C).

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • Cartilage is mixed into some shop tsukune for crunch. It is left out here.
  • A brush of tare at the end adds more soy and wheat, which are already in the mix.

Pairings

  • rice
  • scallion
  • tare
