Grilled Peaches

Next stop: Banana Pudding · continuing the voyage

Warm, juicy peaches with a little char and no glaze.

5 min prep · 8 min cook · Serves 4 · about 4 servings

History

Ripe peaches on a hot grill are a short Southern side or dessert when the fruit is in season, especially in peach-growing parts of Georgia. This version uses fruit, a pinch of salt, and a little oil. There is no honey and no butter, so it stays vegan and dairy-free. The cook is only long enough to mark the cut side.

Cultural background

Grilled ripe peaches with no honey and no butter. A hot-grill fruit side. Vegetarian and vegan as written.

Why it tastes this way

Heat concentrates the juice and browns the cut face, which makes a ripe peach taste fuller without a sauce. Georgia's summer crop is the reason the fruit is on a pit table at all. A pinch of salt keeps it from tasting only sweet. Butter or honey would be a different dish.

How it is made

Halve ripe peaches and grill them cut side down until marked and hot.

Authentic touch

Use peaches that are ripe but still firm. A mushy peach falls through the grate, and a hard one never juices.

How it is used

Warm, as a side or a plain dessert. No ice cream is in this recipe.

Field notes

  • These peaches are grilled with oil and salt only. There is no honey and no butter.
  • The cook is a few minutes. The fruit is not smoked for hours.

Ingredients

  • 4 ripe but firm peaches, halved and pitted
  • 1 teaspoon neutral oil
  • 1 pinch kosher salt
  • A clean grill grate

Steps

  1. Brush the cut faces with oil and salt them lightly. Do not add honey or butter.
  2. Heat a grill hot and oil the grate.
  3. Grill cut side down until marked and the center of the fruit is hot, a few minutes, around 160°F (71°C) if you measure it. Fruit has no meat safety line. Turn them briefly if you want the skin side warm.
  4. Serve warm. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.

These ingredients are vegetables, bread, or a sauce. Keep them clean, and refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours. This is not a canning recipe.

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A pat of butter on the hot fruit adds dairy and removes the vegan and dairy-free claims.

Pairings

  • pork chops
  • chicken
  • ice cream
