Chicken Tikka

Next stop: Seekh Kebab · continuing the voyage

Pronounced CHI-ken TIH-kah

Boneless chicken, tangy from yogurt and warm with cumin and chili.

30 min prep · 20 min cook · Serves 4 · about 4 servings

History

Chicken tikka is boneless chicken in pieces, cooked on skewers in a tandoor or over a hot grill. It is a different plate from tandoori chicken, which is the whole bird. The marinade is the same family of yogurt, chili, and ginger used across Punjabi tandoor cooking. A home grill is a fair stand-in if you say it is a grill.

Cultural background

These are cubes of thigh, not a butterflied chicken and not a creamy malai tikka. Yogurt means dairy, so the dish is not vegan. It belongs to Punjabi and North Indian grill practice.

Why it tastes this way

Small pieces brown fast, so the yogurt marinade chars at the edge while the thigh stays juicy. Cumin and chili sit on the surface instead of hiding inside a whole bird. The taste is the high heat of a tandoor or a hot grate, which is why a crowded, cool pan will not taste the same.

How it is made

Marinate boneless chicken thighs in yogurt and spices, thread them on skewers, and cook them in a tandoor or on a hot grill.

Authentic touch

Use thigh meat. Breast cubes dry out before the marinade has a chance to color.

How it is used

Slide the pieces off the skewers and eat them with onion, lemon, and a spoon of the yogurt you held back from the raw marinade.

Field notes

  • Chicken tikka is boneless pieces. It is a different dish from whole tandoori chicken.
  • Thigh meat stays moister on a hot skewer than lean breast meat does.

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds boneless chicken thighs, cut in 1.5-inch cubes
  • 3/4 cup plain yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon Kashmiri chili powder
  • 1 tablespoon grated ginger
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon neutral oil
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice

Steps

  1. Mix the yogurt, chili, ginger, garlic, cumin, salt, oil, and lemon. Coat the chicken and refrigerate it for at least 30 minutes. Do not leave garlic in oil at room temperature. The meat is still raw. Cook poultry to 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part, not touching bone.
  2. Thread the cubes on skewers, leaving a little space between pieces. Heat a tandoor, or heat a grill until the grate is very hot and say so if it is a grill. Cook poultry to 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part, not touching bone.
  3. Cook, turning, until the center of the thickest cube reaches 165°F (74°C). Do not serve the chicken rare. Cook poultry to 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part, not touching bone.
  4. Rest the skewers a few minutes and serve them hot. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours. Cook poultry to 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part, not touching bone.

Cook poultry to 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part, not touching bone.

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours and use within 3 to 4 days, or freeze. Reheat leftovers to 165°F (74°C). This is a home recipe, not professional food-safety advice.

Variations

  • A hot grill replaces a tandoor if you name it as a grill.
  • A little extra chili makes the edge hotter. This batch stays medium.

Pairings

  • sliced onion
  • lemon
  • cucumber
